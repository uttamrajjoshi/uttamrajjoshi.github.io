About Me

I am a healthcare professional with an academic background in General Medicine and practical experience in hospital-based emergency care. I previously served as a Health Assistant in the Department of Emergency Medicine at Tribhuvan University Teaching Hospital (TUTH), Kathmandu, where I gained experience in emergency and trauma care, patient assessment, clinical procedures and multidisciplinary healthcare services.

Currently, I am pursuing a Bachelor of Public Health (BPH) at the Institute of Medicine (IOM), Tribhuvan University, as a full-scholarship student. This academic journey is helping me connect clinical knowledge with broader perspectives on population health, disease prevention, health promotion and healthcare systems.

My interests include public health, epidemiology, health systems and policy, emergency preparedness, community health and evidence-based practice.

I aspire to contribute to improving healthcare delivery and strengthening community health through the integration of clinical experience, public health knowledge and lifelong learning.

About Me

I am Uttam Raj Joshi, a health professional and Bachelor of Public Health (BPH) student at the Central Department of Public Health, Institute of Medicine, Tribhuvan University, Nepal, pursuing my studies on a full scholarship.

My professional journey includes experience in the Department of Emergency Medicine at Tribhuvan University Teaching Hospital, Kathmandu. This experience has helped shape my understanding of patient care, emergency services and the importance of accessible, quality healthcare.

I am committed to continuous learning and to contributing to public health through education, research and community health initiatives.
